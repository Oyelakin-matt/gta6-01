Solara Bay — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6. A sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a 0–5 star wanted system and police chases, four story missions from fixer Mara Voss, pistol combat, a HUD with a live minimap, start/pause/busted screens, saves, and fully synthesised sound. Everything is original: the city, characters, vehicles, names and story. It runs offline in a desktop browser from static files, built on three.js (vendored).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse to look (click to lock), E to enter a car or answer the phone, click to fire, R to reload, Space for the handbrake, Shift to sprint, Esc to pause, M to mute.
